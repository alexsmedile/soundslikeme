# Voice DNA

## Build it (once)

1. Ask me for 5-10 things I wrote or said myself: captions, DMs, transcripts of my reels, emails. Nothing AI-written.
2. Pull out my voice and save it to voice.md:
   - sentence length and rhythm (typical, shortest, longest)
   - words and phrases I use a lot, and words I never use
   - how I open and how I close
   - humour, slang, emoji, punctuation, capital letters
   - point of view (I / you / we) and how direct I am
   - how I explain things: examples, stories or numbers
   - 5 sentences that are very "me"
3. Show me voice.md and ask what's off. Fix it.

## Use it

Rewrite the draft following voice.md. Then check: would a friend recognise this as me? Mark any line that doesn't sound like me and fix it.
